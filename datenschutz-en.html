Privacy Policy for Mirror

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This is a translation of the German original (Datenschutzerklärung). If anything differs, the German version applies.

In short

  • Mirror stores your entries only on your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud.
  • We can't see your intentions, reflections or notes.
  • The one exception is the optional feature "Mirror thinks along". Only if you allow it, your intention for the day and your answers in the reflection are sent to Anthropic for processing.
  • No tracking, no advertising, no analytics tools.

2. What data Mirror processes

2.1 Storage on your iPhone

Everything you enter in Mirror is stored locally on your device:

  • Answers from onboarding and your starting point
  • Your signature under the agreement
  • Intentions, midday checks and reflections
  • Your own principles, supports and notes
  • Settings (reminder times, appearance, language, Face ID lock)

This data does not leave your iPhone, unless you use "Mirror thinks along" (section 3) or export it yourself (section 2.4). We have no access to it.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app you want to use). To the extent your entries contain information about your health or well-being, you process them yourself on your own device.

If you delete the app or choose "Delete all data" in settings, the data is removed from your iPhone. This cannot be undone.

Note: If iCloud backup is enabled for your iPhone, app data can be part of that backup. Apple's privacy policy applies to it.

2.2 Reminders (notifications)

If you allow reminders, Mirror schedules them directly on your iPhone. The midday reminder can quote your intention for the day. No data is sent to a server for this. You can turn notifications off at any time in Mirror or in iOS settings.

2.3 Face ID lock

If you turn the lock on, iOS checks your face. Mirror only receives the result "recognized" or "not recognized". Your biometric data stays with Apple on your device, and Mirror has no access to it.

2.4 Export and backup

You can export your entries as a PDF or create a backup. The file is only created when you trigger it, and it ends up where you save or send it (for example the Files app or email). From then on, the terms of that service apply.

3. "Mirror thinks along" (AI feature)

What happens

In the reflection, Mirror can ask you fitting questions and show a short mirror of your day. For this, the following is sent to Anthropic's AI model Claude: your intention for the day, your answer to whether you did it, and your answers in the current reflection. The response is sent back to your iPhone.

Earlier entries, notes and principles are not sent, nor is your name or any account. Technically, your IP address is processed when the connection is made.

With every request, the app sends a random identifier that is generated only for your installation (install ID). It doesn't reveal who you are. Our server stores it together with a counter for at most 48 hours, to enforce a daily limit and prevent abuse. No content is stored in the process.

For the same reason, our server counts requests per IP address. It never stores the IP address itself, only a pseudonymized value (a hash with a random value that is newly generated every day), together with a counter, for at most 48 hours.

The legal basis for both is our legitimate interest in secure and affordable operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Only with your consent

The feature is only active if you expressly agree before your first reflection. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and, because reflections can contain information about health or well-being, Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR.

You can withdraw your consent at any time in settings (turn "Mirror thinks along" off). Mirror then keeps working without AI. The withdrawal applies for the future.

Who processes the data

  1. Cloudflare, Inc., San Francisco, USA. Our server (a Cloudflare Worker) forwards the request to Anthropic. It stores no content, only the install ID and the pseudonymized IP value with counters (see above).
  2. Anthropic PBC, San Francisco, USA. Anthropic generates the response. According to Anthropic, inputs and outputs are deleted automatically within 30 days. Longer only if needed to enforce its usage policies or to meet legal obligations. The content is not used to train AI models.

Both process the data on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR). Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

4. Beta via TestFlight

While Mirror is distributed as a beta through Apple TestFlight, Apple collects its own data, for example crash reports and feedback you send through TestFlight. We receive crash reports, technical device information and your feedback, including screenshots you choose to attach. We use these only to fix bugs (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Apple's privacy policy applies to TestFlight.

5. Contact by email

If you write to us, we process your email address and your message in order to reply (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We delete them once the request is dealt with and no retention obligation applies.

6. No tracking

Mirror uses no advertising, no analytics or tracking services, and doesn't sell data.

7. Age

Mirror is intended for people aged 16 and over.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR), as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

Because your entries are stored only on your iPhone, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app: view, edit, export, delete. For anything else, write to themirrorapp@gmx.de.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example the one responsible for us: Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW), Düsseldorf.

If Mirror changes, we will update this policy. The current version at this address applies.
